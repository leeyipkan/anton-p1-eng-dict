Three things that help more than extra practice

  • Read the sentence aloud together the night before — half the marks go on words that were never said out loud.
  • Punctuation is free marks: a capital at the start, a full stop at the end, “?” for questions. He can check these himself.
  • Never practise a tired child. Twelve good minutes beats forty bad ones.
