Ten minutes, four steps

This is the whole method. Nothing else is needed.

  1. Say it. He says the word out loud before he writes. Dictation is hearing first, letters second.
  2. Cover it. Hand over the word. If he can still see it, he is copying.
  3. Write it. One clean try, no fixing letters halfway.
  4. Check it. Compare letter by letter. Tick or cross honestly, then write the miss three times.
